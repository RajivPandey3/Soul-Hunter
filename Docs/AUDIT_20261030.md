## 2. The Level 5 jolt

`run-20261030-postfix` had three running frames of 31-34 ms on Level 5 from 7:50.

Measured since (release builds; Level 5 from 7:50; fixed loadout; 3 min each; save unchanged):
- **`run-20261030-l5jolt`:** 11 runs. Every runtime Update was timed (suite variant `ftwrap`), with loop timing, frame timing, hit timing and trigger counts.
  - Run 1 played while the audit's Unity was importing on the same laptop. Its jolts were rendering and presentation waits under CPU contention, so it is not counted.
  - Of the other 10 runs, 9 had no running frame over 25 ms; their slowest was 17.4-17.8 ms.
  - Run 8 had one frame of 197 ms at 9:33. It was all main thread, inside `AutoAttackWeapon.Update` (195 ms), with 30 enemies, no GC and the gem cap full. It came 2.5 s after a Soul Lantern dropped Hellfire Breath.
- **`run-20261030-breath`:** 3 runs, the bot giving Hellfire Breath at 30 s and then every 40 s (12 breaths, 3 of them first uses). There was no running frame over 25 ms; the slowest was 17.4 ms.

**Cause not proven.** Neither the 31-34 ms frames nor the 197 ms frame came back on purpose. The code-side review (`agent-hotpath.md`) ranks fresh enemy bodies built at a minute change as the most likely cause; that was not measured.

Added so the next one names itself:
- every weapon's attack is timed by type and part (`<Weapon>.Pose`, `.Attack`, `.AfterAttack` in `AutoAttackWeapon.Update`);
- Hellfire Breath's template, flame and aim are timed;
- bot option `-sh-autoplay-breath S` (with `-sh-autoplay-breath-every N`).

- **Graphics options.**

  - The 60 FPS cap cannot be changed (owner decision 2026-10-13).
- **Build contents.** The bot and benchmark are in the release build, and launch flags can turn on god mode, but only on a throw-away save. The 10 showcase scenes are also in the build.
- **Assembly rule.** `UI/HUD` compiles into the Gameplay assembly through an `.asmref`, so the "Gameplay must not depend on UI" rule is not enforced there.
