# Soul Hunter - Master Game Design Document (GDD)

## The Premise & The Twist
* **Protagonist:** Kael
* **The Goal:** Kael makes a pact with the Death God to harvest 100,000 souls from the underworld to bring his dead daughter back to life.
* **The Twist (Level 10):** Upon collecting the final soul, the mirror of truth is revealed. The Death God was Kael himself. His obsession with harvesting souls corrupted him entirely, trapping him in purgatory. The final boss is his own shadow. To free his daughter's soul, he must destroy himself.

---

## 10-Level Blueprint & Mechanics

### Level 1: The Cursed Graveyard
* **Story:** Kael starts his journey near his daughter's grave, harvesting wandering souls.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Dash / Dodge:** Press Shift to dash through enemies and avoid damage.
  * **Soul Burst:** Collect enough souls to fill the ultimate meter. Press Q to trigger a massive screen-clearing explosion.

### Level 2: The Dark Forest
* **Story:** The souls run in fear. Kael realizes the forest is rejecting his dark presence.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Day/Night Cycle:** The map gets progressively darker.
  * **Light Sources:** Enemies are faster in the dark; player must fight near bonfires/torches.

### Level 3: The Forgotten Village
* **Story:** Kael encounters the remnants of a village burned down by demons.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Wandering Merchant:** A mysterious NPC appears mid-game to sell unique permanent upgrades for souls.

### Level 4: The Ruined Castle
* **Story:** The gateway to the Underworld. The Gatekeeper warns Kael that he is being deceived.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Environmental Traps:** Fire breathing gargoyles and spike traps that damage both player and enemies.

### Level 5: The Crimson Swamp
* **Story:** Kael's soul begins to rot. He hears whispers and voices.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Poison Pools:** Toxic puddles on the ground that drain health over time.

### Level 6: The Frozen Peaks
* **Story:** The icy descent into the deep underworld. The cold reflects Kael's fading humanity.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Blizzard:** Periodic snowstorms that heavily slow down player movement. 

### Level 7: The Sea of Lost Souls
* **Story:** The resting place of souls who gave up. Kael starts losing his sanity.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Ghost Enemies:** Intangible enemies that can only be damaged by specific weapons (like Holy Water or Magic).

### Level 8: The Blood Arenas
* **Story:** The Death God's elite guards test Kael's worthiness.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Boss Rush:** No small enemies, only continuous waves of Elite Bosses.

### Level 9: The Throne of Death
* **Story:** The 99,999th soul is collected. Kael approaches the final throne.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Closing Arena:** The safe zone continuously shrinks. The player must survive in extremely tight spaces.

### Level 10: The Soul Core (The Climax)
* **Story:** The truth is revealed. Kael is the monster. He must fight his own corrupted soul.
* **New Mechanics:** 
  * **Mirror Match:** The Final Boss (Shadow Kael) uses the exact same weapons, dash, and upgrades that the player has equipped. It requires pure skill to defeat.
