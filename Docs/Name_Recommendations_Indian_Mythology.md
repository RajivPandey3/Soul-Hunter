# Soul Hunter: Indian mythology names (recommendation)

Owner (2026-10-02): "sabhi ka naam indian mithology ke anusar rakhna hai per abhi nahi game final ho jane ke baad badal dena, per tum as a recommendation pura naam mujhe bana kar dikhaoge".

**Status: recommendation only. Nothing in the game is renamed yet.** The names go in once the game is final and the owner approves this list.

## Rules I followed

- **One idea per item.** Each name says what the item does: a returning weapon becomes a chakram, the revival becomes amrit, the magnet becomes a lodestone. A player should be able to guess the effect.
- **Weapons, artefacts, places and ideas, not gods.** Items are named after mythological weapons, objects, places and concepts, never after a deity directly. Soul Hunter is a dark game, and a deity's name on a cursed item could hurt players. Names that refer to a story (Dadhichi, Vaitarani, Rahu) use the story itself, not worship.
- **Easy to say.** Short Hindi/Sanskrit names, written in plain Roman letters, with the meaning beside each.
- **Evolutions grow from their base names.** For example, Chakram evolves into Sudarshan, and Naagpaash into Rakta Naagpaash.
- **The owner's story stays.** Story characters and places (Kael and the 10 levels) are the owner's GDD and are not renamed. The other playable characters are listed at the end as optional.

## When the game is final

- Names live in one place each:
  - item names in the `WeaponDefinition` assets (`DisplayName`);
  - evolutions in `WeaponEvolutionData.DisplayName`;
  - PowerUps in `PowerUps`;
  - pickups and modes in their scripts.
- Renaming is a data pass with the existing tests, about one cycle.
- Before the change, the owner approves this list (or edits it). After the change, a CTO pass checks every screen for text that no longer fits.
